# Which segments to run: law firms, the general campaign, manufacturing, customer service

22 Nov 2026. Based on the first 5 days of paid data (Meta 17–22 Nov, Google 18–22 Nov), the site content, and web research. Market figures are from secondary sources (trade press, directories, vendor blogs) and are marked as estimates.

## Bottom line

| Segment | Verdict | When |
|---|---|---|
| **AI for business (general)** | **Keep.** It's the only campaign with real search volume and the cheapest landing-page views on Meta. Fix its traffic quality; don't cut it. | Now |
| **Manufacturing** | **No paid campaign.** There isn't one today, only the `/for/manufacturing` page. Keep it that way: keep the page for referrals and LinkedIn outreach. | Revisit in Q1 |
| **Law firms** | **Yes, as a small Meta test.** The new Bar ethics opinion gives you a sharp, timely hook, and copy, images and the landing page already exist. | Start after the 29 Nov review, around 1 Dec |
| **Customer-service companies** | **No segment campaign.** Use customer service as an angle (a hook) inside the general campaign. Approach call-centre outsourcers as partners, not as ad targets. | Hook now, outreach optional |

The bigger risk right now isn't the segment mix. Two leaks come first: **about half of all clicks never load the page** (Meta: 559 clicks, 277 landing page views), and **the Meta Lead event is empty**. Every extra segment you add before fixing them spreads the same leak across more budget.

No segment is near that yet, so the decision on general vs. vertical belongs to the review on about 13 Dec, not today.

## 2. The general campaign ("AI for business"): keep it

**For it**
- It's the only place with search demand. Keyword Planner showed about 500 searches a month each for "בינה מלאכותית לעסקים", "אוטומציה לעסקים" and "סוכן AI", against under 10 for each domain phrase.
- On Meta it gets the most traffic at the lowest cost per landing view (₪1.19).
- It matches the 22 Nov repositioning ("Your business can work smarter", home page) better than any vertical page does.

**Against it**
- Broad AI intent attracts course-seekers, tool-shoppers and news readers (the Anthropic IPO, product names). It's the source of most of the Google junk.
- It lands on the home page, which is less specific than a `/for/` page, so expect a lower conversion rate per click.

**What to do**
- Google: keep only the exact-match set added on 22 Nov. Watch impressions: if exact match barely serves, add phrase match back one term at a time, never broad.
- Meta: keep it at ₪30/day. `hook-tools` took 68% of its spend (₪120). Make sure the paused hooks are replaced with one or two new angles so it isn't a one-ad campaign. A customer-service hook is the best candidate (section 5).
- Update the Meta and Google copy still using the pre-22 Nov headline. Memory flags this as open.

## 3. Manufacturing: there's no campaign, and don't add one yet

There's no manufacturing campaign on Google or Meta. The repo has only a landing page (`/for/manufacturing`) and the "quotes depend on a few experienced people" example on `/business-use-case`. That was the plan from 18 Nov ("law, manufacturing, e-commerce = referrals only"), and it still holds:

- **Search intent is wrong.** Industrial searches in Hebrew are about PLCs, robotics and control systems. The "AI for business" campaign already negates "תעשייתית", "plc", "רובוטיקה" and "בקרה" because they brought those searchers in.
- **Meta can't find the buyer.** Plant managers and owners of mid-size manufacturers don't show up as a Meta interest, and the ₪30/day ad sets would spend on "industry" enthusiasts.
- **The sale is slow and needs a relationship.** Manufacturers buy on referral and site visits. LinkedIn outreach to COOs and plant managers, plus industry bodies (the Manufacturers Association, regional industrial parks), suit it better than paid social.

Keep the page. Use it as the link in LinkedIn messages and referrals. Reconsider a LinkedIn campaign in Q1, once the Insight Tag is live.

## 4. Law firms: yes, as a controlled test

**Why it's worth testing**
- **A large, dense market.** The Israel Bar Association has over 80,000 members, one of the highest lawyer-per-capita ratios anywhere. Most firms are small (up to 9 lawyers), and those firms led last year's pay rises, so there's money and margin pressure.
- **A new, specific pain, as of September 2026.** On 17 Sep 2026 the Bar's National Ethics Committee published an updated opinion on AI. As reported, it says:
  - AI agents can't make substantive legal decisions without close human supervision.
  - Lawyers must verify every output independently.
  - Privileged or personal data can't go into "open" AI systems.
  - A general "we may use AI tools" clause in the fee agreement isn't enough.
  - Lawyers need basic AI literacy.

  Courts have already sanctioned lawyers for AI misuse. That turns "we should look at AI" into "we need a compliant setup", which is exactly a Decision Sprint: choose tools, write the policy, set up the workflow.
- **Search signals already exist.** "תקדין ai" and "דין רגע" (Israeli legal-AI products) showed up in the 22 Nov search terms. Lawyers are already looking.
- **It costs almost nothing to launch.** Hooks (`search`, `drafts`, `privilege`), banners in all three sizes, and the `/for/law` page already exist.

**Why to keep it small**
- **There's a lot of competition, and it's product-led.** Techdin AI (Hebrew research over 7M+ documents), Din Rega (research and drafting), LitiGate (litigation), Lizzy AI (secure GenAI), plus Nevo partnering with the Legal Mind community on firm training. The Bar runs its own AI CPD courses. Lawyers can get a free demo from a vendor. Your angle has to be **"independent: which of these tools, set up compliantly, inside your firm"**. Never position as another tool.
- **They're slow, sceptical buyers.** Partners delegate little and dislike being sold to. Expect a higher cost per lead than accounting.
- **There are claim risks.** Don't paraphrase the Bar opinion in an ad unless you've read the primary text. Sources disagree on what's binding: one reports binding guidance in April, others call the guidance non-binding. Never imply that bee & co. gives ethics or legal advice.
- **A conflicting negative keyword.** "AI for business" negates "משפטי". That's fine for the general campaign, but law needs its own campaign, not an ad group inside general.

**The test**
- **Meta:** one ad set, "Law – Israel 30–65", with interests in law firms, attorney and legal practice, ₪30/day, for 2 weeks (about ₪420).
- **Ads:** `law-hook-privilege` first, since it's closest to the September opinion. Rework its subline into something like "מה מותר ומה אסור אחרי חוות הדעת של הלשכה, ואיך מסדרים את זה במשרד", after you've checked the primary text. Run `law-hook-drafts` and `law-headline` alongside it.
- **Link:** `/for/law?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=law-w2&utm_content={angle}`. Add a short line on the page that mentions the opinion, so the ad and page match.
- **Google:** check Keyword Planner first: "בינה מלאכותית לעורכי דין", "AI לעורכי דין", "AI למשרד עורכי דין", "כלי AI לעורכי דין". Run them exact match, ₪20/day, only if they show 50+ searches a month.
- **Pass/fail on about 15 Dec:** at least 2 leads, or a cost per landing view under ₪2 with at least 1 booked call, means continue. Otherwise pause it and keep law on referrals.
- **Funding:** take it from Meta Distribution if it still has no leads at the 29 Nov review. Don't add it on top of the budget.

## 5. Customer-service companies: an angle, not a segment

This can mean two different audiences.

**a) Companies whose business is customer service (call-centre and BPO outsourcers).** Don't run ads to them.
- It's a tiny market. Directories list only a handful of Israeli outsourcers (Goodfirms: 7; the largest found was Kishurit, with 250–999 staff).
- They're being disrupted by AI themselves. They buy voice-AI from product vendors such as the Hebrew voice-agent sellers, which claim one AI agent does the work of 3–5 reps, against ₪8–12k a month per rep (vendor figures). For you they're closer to **partners or a channel** than customers: they could resell an "AI-assisted service desk" setup to their clients.
- If you want to try it: 5–10 direct LinkedIn messages to their COOs or heads of operations. That's free, and you'll learn quickly.

**b) Ordinary companies with a big service team** (e-commerce, distribution, insurance agencies, utilities). This is a strong use case, and it's already on the site: "Reps search for answers instead of helping customers" on `/business-use-case`, and the customer-service drafts idea on the distribution page.
- **Use it as a hook in the general Meta campaign**, for example "הנציגים מחפשים תשובות במקום לענות ללקוחות?" with a subline about drafts from order and shipping data that a rep approves.
- **Avoid it on Google.** Searches for "בוט שירות לקוחות", "צ'אטבוט" and "וואטסאפ" pull in cheap-bot shoppers. That's why "בוט", "צ'אטבוט" and "וואטסאפ" are already negatives, and it contradicts the premium, executive positioning.

## 6. Suggested sequence

1. **This week:** fix the mobile load issue (only half of clicks load the page), test the Meta Lead event, and add the customer-service hook to Meta "AI business".
2. **29 Nov review:** same exports as before. Decide where the ₪30/day for law comes from.
3. **About 1 Dec:** launch the Meta law test (and Google law only if Keyword Planner shows volume). Read the primary Bar opinion before you finalise the copy.
4. **About 13–15 Dec:** first real segment decision: general vs. accounting vs. distribution vs. law, on cost per booked call. Manufacturing stays on referrals and LinkedIn.

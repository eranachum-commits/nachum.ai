# bee & co. wave 1: creative, campaign setup and 8-week paid plan

Read-only review of `marketing/ads/*`, `brand/*`, `public/og/*`, `scripts/make-ad-files.mjs`, the `lp` and `industries` content, and `public/js/app.js` (tracking). I did not fetch the live page. Every benchmark below is an **estimate** unless marked otherwise.

## Bottom line
- The message is right: the question headlines match each landing page's H1 word for word, and the "Build / Not now / Don't build" memo is a real differentiator.
- The executions are weak in the feed. Pale sage background, light type weights, 30–45% empty canvas, no face. In a founder-led offer, Eran's face is the strongest thing you have, and no ad shows it.
- **Two blockers come before spending:**
  1. LinkedIn has no copy, no Insight Tag and no campaign structure in the repo, even though it is a priority channel.
  2. `app.js` hard-codes `ad_storage: 'denied'` even after consent. Combined with no Meta pixel or Insight Tag, that means no retargeting pools, no conversion optimisation, and Google Ads attribution that is probably undercounted.

## 2. Ad copy and structure

**Google keywords.** Expect several of these phrase-match Hebrew terms to show "low search volume" and never serve. My estimate is tens of searches a month or fewer per term.

Terms to add:
- Accounting: "סריקת חשבוניות אוטומטית", "פענוח חשבוניות", "OCR חשבוניות", "קליטת חשבוניות ספקים", "בינה מלאכותית במשרד רואי חשבון", "RPA הנהלת חשבונות".
- Distribution: "קליטת הזמנות מהמייל", "הזמנות PDF ל-ERP", "בינה מלאכותית ב-ERP", "AI ליבואן", "אוטומציה לסיטונאות".
- Optional third campaign (10–15% of Google budget) for the end-to-end positioning: "ייעוץ AI לעסקים", "הטמעת בינה מלאכותית בעסק", "יועץ בינה מלאכותית". Send it to the home page and expect higher CPCs and course-seeker traffic.

**Negatives.** These gaps will waste budget:
- **"הקלדת הזמנות" is a job-seeker magnet.** Add עבודה, עבודות, מהבית, מהבית בתשלום.
- **"אוטומציה להזמנות" catches appointment and restaurant bookings.** Add תורים, תור, מסעדה, מלון, אירוע, Shopify, וולט, אתר.
- For accounting: עוסק פטור, עוסק מורשה, אפליקציה, חשבונית ירוקה, מורנינג, ChatGPT, פרומפט, סדנה, הכשרה, מדריך, וובינר, מה זה.
- Keep "שכר" (salary) but watch it: the accounting page names payroll as a next process.

**RSAs.**
- Each ad has only 8 of 15 headlines and 3 of 4 descriptions, so Ad Strength will be weak. Add seven headlines per ad, for example "בלי להחליף את התוכנה", "מדיניות AI למשרד", "קודם החלטה, אחר כך בנייה", "מה לבנות ומה לא", "ספרינט החלטה במחיר קבוע", "סודיות לקוחות מובנית", "תשובה כנה כבר בשיחה".
- **Disapproval risk:** "עובד עם Priority ו-SAP B1" uses a trademark in ad text and implies a partnership. Replace with **"מתחברים ל-ERP שכבר יש לכם"** ("We connect to the ERP you already have"). Keywords containing those names are fine.
- "מבט עצמאי ובכיר" praises itself. Use **"מבט עצמאי, לא של ספק: מה שווה לאוטמט ומה לא."** ("An independent view, not a vendor's: what's worth automating and what isn't.")
- No assets are configured. Add sitelinks (איך זה עובד, על ערן, שאלות נפוצות, שיחה של 30 דקות), callouts (בלי אנשי מכירות, מחיר קבוע לספרינט, סודיות מובנית) and a "Services" structured snippet.

**Schedule and bids.**
- Sun–Thu 08:00–19:00 is too tight for owners. Run Sun–Thu 07:00–22:00 plus Friday 08:00–13:00, then trim using data from weeks 3–4.
- Max clicks with a ₪8 cap is fine. Estimated CPCs are ₪5–15. Stay on it: at this volume Smart Bidding won't get the roughly 15–30 conversions a month it needs.

**Meta copy.**
- The "what not to build" ad titles don't match the image: "שותף אחד מקצה לקצה" and "מהמייל ישר ל-ERP". Change both to **"מה לבנות, ומה לא"**.
- The body switches to first person ("הייתי ממליץ") while the brand speaks as "we". Pick founder voice and keep it consistently.
- "רעיונות שמשרדי רואי חשבון מעלים" hints at sector experience. Frame it as method instead: **"ככה נראית החלטת AI לפני שמשקיעים שקל: מה לבנות, מה לא עכשיו ומה בכלל לא."** ("This is what an AI decision looks like before you spend a shekel: build, not now, never.")
- Optimising for Traffic/link clicks will buy accidental clicks. Switch to landing page views as soon as the pixel is live, then to leads.

**LinkedIn copy is missing. Drafts for Thought Leader ads from Eran's profile:**
- Accounting: **"כשמשרד רואי חשבון שואל על AI, הרעיון הראשון הוא בדרך כלל בוט ללקוחות. אני הייתי מתחיל ממקום אחר: המיון וההתאמות שאוכלים לצוות את העונה. בשיחה של 30 דקות נבדוק אם יש אצלכם משהו ששווה לבנות, ומה לא."** (Firms usually start with a client bot; I'd start with the sorting and reconciliations that eat the season. 30 minutes to see what's worth building, and what isn't.)
- Distribution: **"אם אצלכם עדיין מקלידים הזמנות מהמייל ל-ERP, זה בדרך כלל המקום הראשון לבדוק. בלי להחליף מערכת. 30 דקות, ותדעו אם זה משתלם."** (If orders are still typed from email into the ERP, that's usually the first place to look. No system switch. 30 minutes and you'll know if it pays.)

**UTMs and tracking gaps.**
1. **No `utm_content` or `utm_term`.** Both Meta ads in each ad set share one URL, so creatives can't be told apart in GA. Add `utm_content={{ad.name}}` on Meta, `utm_term={keyword}&utm_content={creative}` on Google, and `utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=paid_social` on LinkedIn.
2. **`ad_storage` stays denied after consent.** Either the banner offers an advertising choice that grants `ad_storage`, `ad_user_data` and `ad_personalization`, or the paid channels stay blind. Add Consent Mode v2 so Google can model conversions from people who decline. The banner wording must match whatever is enabled.
3. **No Meta pixel or LinkedIn Insight Tag.** Without them there are no retargeting audiences. Install both behind the same consent.
4. **"Booked call" is only `booking_link_click`**, a click on the calendar link, not a confirmed booking. The source of truth has to be a lead log Eran keeps by hand. The form already stores `location.search` in `source`, so the gclid survives. Upload a monthly Google Ads offline conversion called `booked_call`.
5. **GA4 sees only leads from visitors who consented.** Reconcile against the lead inbox, which is the system of record.

## 3. 8-week paid plan

Percentages are of the total paid budget; I haven't assumed a total.

| Weeks | Google | LinkedIn | Meta | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (pre-launch) | — | — | — | Tracking fixes, lead log, creative fixes, LinkedIn build |
| 1–2 | 50% | 50% | 0% | Search intent plus LinkedIn reach to the job titles; pixels collect audiences |
| 3–4 | 40% | 40% | 20% | Meta retargeting (site visitors, 30 days) plus a small Meta cold test |
| 5–8 | Rebalanced | Rebalanced | Rebalanced | Shift 10–15 points a week toward the lowest cost per booked call; keep at least 15% on any channel still producing calls |

**Targeting**
- **LinkedIn**
  - Accounting audience: Israel; industry Accounting; seniority Owner/Partner/CXO; company size 2–200.
  - Distribution audience: industries Wholesale / Import & Export / Distribution; titles CEO, COO, CFO, owner, Operations Manager; company size 11–500.
  - Expect audiences in the low thousands (estimate). Turn off Audience Expansion and LinkedIn Audience Network.
  - Formats: (a) Thought Leader ads, the primary bet; (b) square single-image ads; (c) a Document Ad of the memo.
  - In week 3, test a Lead Gen Form (name, email, company, situation) against the site form. Expect more leads at lower quality.
- **Meta:** age 30–65, feeds only. Cold interest targeting in Israel is thin, so treat it as a test. From week 3, retarget landing-page visitors who didn't submit (pages viewed, 30 days) with the memo creative and a direct "30 minutes with Eran" CTA.
- **Google:** the two domain campaigns plus the optional generic campaign. Add negatives weekly.

**Creative testing**, one variable at a time per channel:
- Weeks 1–2: current headline against the new evergreen headline.
- Weeks 3–4: the winner with and without Eran's photo.
- Weeks 5–6: the headline angle against the memo angle.
- Weeks 7–8: dark against light background.

At this volume no test will reach statistical significance. Call a winner on CTR plus lead count after the loser has spent about 1×T (T is defined below).

**KPIs.** All of these come from the lead log, by utm.
- CPL = spend ÷ leads.
- Lead→call rate = calls held ÷ leads. A healthy rate is around 40–60% (estimate).
- **Cost per booked call (CPBC) = spend ÷ calls held.** This is the north-star metric.
- Call→Sprint rate = sprints sold ÷ calls held.
- Leading indicators: CTR (estimates: Google above 5%, LinkedIn above 0.5%, Meta above 0.8%) and landing-page form-start rate (`booking_initiated` ÷ sessions).

**Decision rules.** Eran sets **T = the maximum acceptable CPBC**, derived as sprint margin × expected call→sprint rate.
- **Kill** an ad group, ad set or creative when it has spent 2×T with zero leads, or 3×T with zero calls.
- **Fix the landing page, not the ads,** when CTR is healthy but form-start is below about 3%.
- **Scale** by 20–30% a week when CPBC is at or below T over a rolling 3-week window with at least 3 calls.
- **Wave 2** (insurance, real estate) launches only once one domain has held CPBC at or below T for 3 weeks.

**What must be true before scaling:**
- Tracking matches the lead log within ±20%.
- At least 3 calls held per channel.
- Eran's calendar can absorb double the current call volume.
- At least one Sprint sold from paid traffic, or a credible pipeline of them.

**Weekly review** (Sunday, 30 minutes):
1. Update the lead log statuses.
2. Go through the Google search-terms report and add negatives.
3. Per channel: spend, CTR, leads, calls, CPBC, and the 3-week rolling CPBC.
4. Apply the kill/scale rules.
5. Choose next week's single creative change.
6. On the first Sunday of the month: Google offline conversion upload.

**Lead-handling SLA.**
- A personal reply from Eran within 4 business hours, Sun–Thu. Leads that arrive after Thursday 14:00 get a reply Friday by 12:00.
- Each reply references the situation the lead picked and includes the calendar link.
- If there's no booking: follow up on day 2 (a short email with one memo insight) and day 6 (the last nudge). Then the lead joins the retargeting pool.
- Aim to hold the call within 5 business days. Log every status the same day.

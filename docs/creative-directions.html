Creative recommendation

Choose Quiet Authority.

It best balances premium consultancy stature with operational clarity. The warm editorial surface feels commissioned rather than templated, while the value ledger makes the transformation method legible to a nontechnical executive in seconds.

It also has the strongest bilingual potential: the composition does not depend on left-to-right diagrams, and the visual hierarchy can mirror cleanly in Hebrew without becoming a second design.

Recommended blend
  1. Use Quiet Authority as the core identity and page grammar.
  2. Borrow the evidence annotations and decision gates from Operating Blueprint.
  3. Reserve Human Signal's dark aubergine atmosphere for the founder story and final consultation CTA.
